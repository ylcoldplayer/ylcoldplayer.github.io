Budget pacing as dual gradient descent

18 September, 2026 in advertising, optimization · Tags: budget pacing, duality, online learning

A recurring question from engineers new to ads systems is why budget pacing controllers all end up looking the same: keep a single scalar, shade every bid by it, and nudge it up or down according to whether you are overspending. The answer is that this is exactly online gradient descent on the dual of a linear program, and the scalar $\lambda$ is the Lagrange multiplier on the budget constraint — which is why the optimal bid is the shaded $b_t = v_t/(1+\lambda)$ and not something else. A contraction you can hear

5 September, 2026 in reinforcement learning · Tags: dynamic programming, policy iteration

Dynamic programming has one theorem that does most of the work, and it is worth being precise about what it does and does not give you. The Bellman operator satisfies $\norm{Tv - Tw}_\infty \le \gamma\norm{v-w}_\infty$, so value iteration converges geometrically; but optimistic policy iteration is a composition of that contraction with a discontinuous greedy selection, and its convergence is a genuinely more delicate question.
