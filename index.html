About

I am a software engineer and researcher working on ads bidding, pacing, and auction systems. My research interests span digital advertising, machine learning, and reinforcement learning.

Earlier I worked in harmonic analysis, on dispersive and Strichartz estimates for the wave equation on Riemannian manifolds of bounded curvature. You can reach me at ychenfd@gmail.com.
